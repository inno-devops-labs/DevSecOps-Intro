### What broke and how we fixed it

`readOnlyRootFilesystem: true` broke Juice Shop because it writes to `/juice-shop/data` (SQLite, static files) and `/juice-shop/ftp`. `emptyDir volumes` masked original files; an `initContainer` to copy them also failed under PSS restricted. Removed `readOnlyRootFilesystem` while keeping all other hardening – PSS restricted does not mandate a read‑only root, so the deployment remains compliant.
