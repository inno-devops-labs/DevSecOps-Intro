# Dispositions

This file records the author’s decision for every finding in `peer-review-received.md`. The actual reviewer’s findings have not yet been provided in this draft, so it would be inaccurate to invent finding IDs or claim that findings were accepted, rejected, or deferred.

For each finding received during Lab 5.7, record one of these dispositions and give a reason tied to evidence:

- **Accepted:** the finding is correct; identify the artifact or control changed and the evidence that demonstrates the change.
- **Rejected:** explain why the finding does not apply and cite the relevant design or test evidence. Disagreement alone is not a reason.
- **Deferred:** explain the dependency or reason for delay, identify the residual risk, assign an owner role, and set a review date.

Every finding in the actual peer-review record must have exactly one corresponding disposition. The completed file should not retain this instruction section once the review exchange has taken place.
